The Solution

What we set up for you

A reply system that works in the background so you never miss the window again.

1

We learn your business

Before we build anything, we spend time understanding your trade, your typical customers, and how you like to communicate. This makes sure every reply sounds like you — not a generic template.

We'll ask you a few simple questions: what jobs you take on, what areas you cover, how you prefer customers to get in touch, and what you'd normally say in a first reply.

Takes: A 20–30 minute call with Reuben. You don't need to prepare anything — just answer naturally.

2

We build and connect your system

We set up a reply system connected directly to your inbox. When a new enquiry lands, the system reads it, understands what the customer is asking about, and sends a personalised reply — all within seconds.

The reply is written in plain, natural language. It thanks them for getting in touch, acknowledges what they need, and invites them to book a time or share more details. It sounds like a real person — because we write it that way.

Takes: 24 hours to set up. We handle everything — you don't log in anywhere or install anything.

3

We test it before it goes live

We run test enquiries through the system to make sure every reply reads naturally and represents your business well. If anything needs adjusting — the tone, the wording, what's included in the response — we fix it before you see a single real customer message.

You review the test replies and give us the go-ahead. Nothing goes live until you're happy with it.

Takes: Usually same day. We send you examples to approve before switching it on.

4

It runs while you work

Once it's live, every new enquiry gets an instant, professional response — whether it's 8am on a Monday or 9pm on a Sunday. The customer feels looked after. They don't go elsewhere. And when you're free, you follow up with a warm lead instead of a cold one.

You don't manage anything. No logins, no dashboards, no tech. It just runs in the background.

Day to day: You work as normal. Enquiries get replied to. You follow up when you're ready. We monitor the system and fix anything if needed.

5

We keep it running and improve it over time

We check in regularly to make sure the system is performing well. If you start getting a new type of enquiry, expand your services, or want the tone adjusted, we update it for you.

Your monthly fee covers everything — the system, the running costs, and ongoing management. Nothing extra, no surprises.

Questions

Things people usually ask

Will customers know it's automated?

No — and that's by design. We spend time learning how you communicate and write the replies to match your natural tone. To a customer, it reads like a message from you or someone on your team.

What if a customer asks something specific about a job?

The system handles general enquiries and gets the conversation started — asking when they're free, what the job involves, and keeping them engaged. Anything that needs a specific answer from you comes to your inbox as normal, with the customer already warm and waiting.

Do I need to change anything about how I work?

Not at all. You keep using your existing email. We connect to it in the background. Your inbox looks the same — you just see replies already sent and warm leads ready for you to follow up.

What if I get a lot of spam enquiries?

We set up filters so the system only responds to genuine customer enquiries. Spam, automated emails, and irrelevant messages are ignored.

How quickly can you get it set up?

Usually within 24 hours of our initial call. Once we understand your business and you've approved the test replies, we switch it on — and it starts working straight away.

What does it cost?

£350 per month, all inclusive. We offer a free 2-week trial so you can see it working before you commit to anything. No setup fee, no contract.
